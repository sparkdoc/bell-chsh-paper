# Original, unreviewed manuscripts (2026-09-02)

These three PDFs are the papers exactly as produced by the local Qwen3.8-27B model under the
author's direction, before the independent review of 2026-09-03.  They are included, unaltered,
so that readers can see precisely what the model produced on its own.  The corrected versions
are in the parent directory; every difference is itemised in `../CHANGELOG.md` and in
`../../review_2026-09-03/REVIEW_REPORT_fable_2026-09-03.md`.  Known defects of these originals
include: the CGLMP target is the maximally-entangled-state value, not the quantum maximum; a
duplicated equation label makes the proof of Theorem 7 cite the wrong equation; the Tsirelson,
Shimony, Jarrett and Vieira et al. bibliography entries are wrong; Barrett-Gisin and Puetz et al.
are not cited; and one sentence in Sec. VII.5 is false in the paper's own notation.
